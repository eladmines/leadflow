# F31 — Website visit counts

Status: **Built** (first version, 2026-11-23). Decision: D-190 (Accepted).

F26 and F30 say which ad brought each _lead_. Without the visits, you can't tell whether a campaign
converts: 3 inquiries from 120 visits is not 3 from 12. F31 counts visits, per source and campaign,
and shows them next to the leads.

## What is counted

A **count**, never a person: `visit_counts` (business × local day × source × name × campaign → count).
No visitor id, no IP, no page list, no click id value.

- **SimpleCatch sites:** each entry from outside the site (a direct visit, another site, an ad). Moving
  between the site's own pages, bots/crawlers (user agent), and browser prefetches don't count.
- **Other websites (F30 script):** one visit per browser session (a `sessionStorage` flag), posted to
  `POST /api/v1/embed/visit` with the same key and the same CORS/CSRF rules as the lead endpoint.
  Per-IP rate limit `EMBED_VISIT_RATE_LIMIT_MAX` (default 60 per window).
- A visit is counted **under the source a lead sent now would get** (an ad click stored in the last 30
  days wins), so a campaign's visits and leads describe the same people.

## Screens

Reports → "Where your customers come from": two new columns per source and per campaign, **Website
visits** and **Became an inquiry** ("3 of 120 (2.5%)"). Shown as a dash when there are no visits or when
leads exceed visits (WhatsApp and Messenger ad leads have no website visit). Rows with visits but no
leads appear too. Direct visits join the "unknown" row, where leads without a source already are.

## Limits (said plainly)

Visits, not unique people; ad blockers and uncounted bots make it approximate (it won't match Google
Analytics exactly); only sites with the script or built in SimpleCatch.

## Guards

At most 300 distinct source/campaign combinations per business per day (junk `utm_campaign=random`
links can't grow the table); a wrong key is a plain 400; reports read only the business's own rows.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Three ad visits and one inquiry from them: the campaign shows 3 visits, 1 inquiry.
2. Bots, a wrong key and garbage create nothing; another business sees none of it.
3. SimpleCatch site: an entry counts once; own-page moves, bots and prefetches don't.
4. No click id value stored; Hebrew and English.

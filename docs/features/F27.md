# F27 — Email conversations

Status: **Built** (first version, 2026-11-10; the real inbound provider and domain are still open). Decision: D-177 (Proposed). Builds on F24 (inbox) and F25 (channels).

A customer's email to the business becomes a conversation and a lead, like WhatsApp (F12) and Messenger
(F23); the team replies from the inbox or the lead page, as the business, from its own address.

## Scope (first version)

- **Forwarding address.** Settings → Channels → Email: the owner presses "Create address" and gets
  `<random>@<EMAIL_INBOUND_DOMAIN>` (a random slug, e.g. `k7m2q9x4pa`), shown with a copy button. They
  put it on the website, in their signature, or forward mail to it. One address per business;
  disconnect deletes it (existing conversations stay). Stored in `channel_connections`
  (`channel = 'email'`, `externalId` = the full address, no token).
- **Inbound webhook** `POST /api/v1/integrations/email/inbound`, public, signed with
  `X-LeadFlow-Signature: sha256=<HMAC of the raw body>` (`EMAIL_INBOUND_SECRET`). The JSON body is
  provider-neutral: `{ to, from, fromName?, subject, text, messageId, inReplyTo?, autoSubmitted?,
receivedAt? }`. A thin adapter per real provider (Brevo / Mailgun / Postmark) will translate its own
  format into this one; until one is chosen the `local` adapter and `scripts/email-simulate.ts` feed it.
  Not configured (`EMAIL_INBOUND_SECRET` unset) → 404.
- **Routing and safety.** The recipient address finds the business (unknown address → ignored, 200).
  Ignored: auto-generated mail (`autoSubmitted`), mail from the business's own address or from the
  inbound domain (loops), the same `messageId` twice (idempotent). Quoted lines (`>`) are cut; the
  text is capped at 4096 characters; HTML is never stored or rendered.
- **Data.** The WhatsApp tables stay channel-aware: `channel = 'email'`; the "number" is
  `mail:<sender address>` (column widened to 260); messages get a nullable `subject`. The contact is
  matched by email within the business (else created from the sender's name or address); lead source
  `email` (enum value added), first message = subject + text.
- **Replies.** From the inbox or the lead page, through the email sender, **from the business's
  forwarding address** (display name = the business name), `Reply-To` the same, `Subject: Re: <last
subject>`, `In-Reply-To` the last message id. No 24-hour window. If the server sends no email
  (`EMAIL_TRANSPORT=disabled`) the reply is refused with a Hebrew explanation.
- **Inbox and lead page.** Conversations from email show the address and subject; the composer has no
  window notice; the lead gets the Email source icon and label (list, filter, reports, export).
- **Notifications.** A new email lead notifies owners/managers like other channels ("ליד חדש ממייל").
- **Not in this version:** attachments, HTML rendering, CC/BCC, several addresses, connecting the
  business's own mailbox (IMAP), automatic reply/booking on email, DKIM/SPF setup (see below).

## Setup (owner / hosting)

- A domain you own (e.g. `mail.example.com`), an inbound-email service pointed at it (MX records) that
  can call the webhook, and the same domain verified for sending (SPF/DKIM) at the email provider so
  replies from `…@mail.example.com` are accepted. Free tiers exist but none is chosen yet (D-177).
- Server settings: `EMAIL_INBOUND_DOMAIN`, `EMAIL_INBOUND_SECRET`; for replies `EMAIL_TRANSPORT` other
  than `disabled`.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Creating the address shows it; creating it twice returns the same one; only the owner can create or
   delete it; without `EMAIL_INBOUND_DOMAIN` the row says email isn't set up on the server.
2. A signed inbound email from a new sender creates a contact and an `email` lead and a conversation
   with the subject and text; a second email joins the same lead.
3. Unsigned or wrongly signed requests are rejected (401) and store nothing; the same `messageId` twice
   stores one message; unknown recipients, auto-submitted mail and loops are ignored.
4. A reply from the inbox or the lead page is sent from the forwarding address with `Reply-To`,
   `Re:` subject and `In-Reply-To`, appears in the thread, and works after any time; with sending
   disabled it's refused in Hebrew without leaking provider details.
5. Tenant isolation and permissions as the other channels (route walker, employees only assigned leads,
   only owners/managers see the inbox). No message texts or addresses in logs.
6. Lead source filter, export and reports include "Email". Hebrew and English copy.

# F28 — Instagram DMs → conversations and leads

Status: **Built** (first version, 2026-11-22). Decision: D-187. Builds on F23 (Messenger), F24 (inbox), F25 (channels).

A direct message to the business's Instagram account becomes a conversation and a lead, like Messenger.

## Scope (first version)

- **Instagram Messaging API with Facebook Login**, in the same Meta app. The business's Instagram
  account must be **professional** (business or Creator) and linked to a Facebook Page it manages.
- **Connecting** (Settings → Channels → Instagram): if the business already connected its Facebook Page,
  its Instagram account is found with that Page's token, with no new sign-in. Otherwise the person signs in
  with Facebook (the same flow as Facebook, asking also for `instagram_basic` and
  `instagram_manage_messages`), and picks one of their Pages that has an Instagram account. The Page token
  is stored sealed (like Facebook); the connection's `externalId` is the Instagram account id, its name
  `@username`.
- **Webhook**: the same URL as Messenger (`/api/v1/integrations/messenger/webhook`), `object: "instagram"`;
  the account id routes the message to its business. Echoes (the account's own messages sent from
  Instagram) are stored as outgoing, as for Facebook.
- **Data**: the channel-aware tables get `channel = instagram`; the sender's "number" is `ig:` + their
  Instagram-scoped id. Inbox, classification, thread, notifications and the lead list work as for
  Messenger and show the channel. New lead source `instagram`; the contact's name is the sender's
  Instagram name/username, else "Instagram user".
- **Replies** within 24 hours of the customer's last message (Send API, `messaging_type: RESPONSE`).
- **Adapters**: the Messenger `local`/`cloud` adapters, with a profile lookup for Instagram.
- Not in this version: story replies and mentions (shown as a label), comments, ads, automatic reply or
  booking on Instagram, several accounts.

## Meta setup (owner)

- Add the **Instagram** product to the Meta app (API setup with Facebook login) and, in its webhook
  settings, the same callback URL and verify token as Messenger; subscribe `messages`.
- The Facebook Login for Business configuration (or the scopes) must include `instagram_basic` and
  `instagram_manage_messages`.
- In Instagram: Settings → Messages and story replies → allow access to messages for connected tools.
- Until App Review, only people with a role in the Meta app (and Instagram testers) can use it.

## Acceptance criteria

1. A signed Instagram event to a connected account lands in that business's conversations, with the
   channel shown; the sender becomes a lead with source "Instagram" once classified as business.
2. A reply from the thread goes out through the Page token within 24 hours; after that the screen explains
   why it can't.
3. The account's own message sent from Instagram (echo) appears in the thread once, never twice.
4. "Connect Instagram" with the Facebook Page already connected needs no sign-in; a Page without an
   Instagram account gets a clear message.
5. Tokens are never returned or logged; an account belongs to one business; tenant isolation as in F25.

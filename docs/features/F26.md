# F26 — Where leads come from (ad and campaign attribution)

Status: **Planned** (owner asked 2026-11-09). Decision: D-162 (_Proposed_).

Every lead says **which source, ad and campaign brought it** — "Google Ads · campaign 'Haircut summer' ·
keyword 'hairdresser Haifa'" — and Reports show which sources and campaigns bring leads **and customers**.
The existing `source` (website form, WhatsApp, Facebook, manual) says _how_ the lead arrived; this adds
_why_ — the marketing behind it.

## What is captured

**Website leads (LeadFlow sites).** When a visitor arrives, the public site server reads, without any
script:

- click ids: `gclid` (Google Ads auto-tagging), `fbclid` (Facebook/Instagram), `ttclid` (TikTok),
  `msclkid` (Microsoft Ads);
- `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`, `utm_term`, `utm_content`;
- the referring site (host only) and the landing page (path, no query).

It keeps them in a first-party cookie on the business's site (`lf_src`, 30 days, `HttpOnly`,
`SameSite=Lax`, values only — no personal data, no visitor id). **A new ad click replaces it; otherwise
the first visit stays.** When the visitor sends the form — on any page — the lead is saved with it.
⚠️ Today's sites set no cookies; this one needs the owner's OK (privacy, D-162).

**WhatsApp leads from Meta ads ("Click to WhatsApp").** Meta's webhook sends a `referral` with the ad
(id, headline, source URL, `ctwa_clid`); the lead keeps it as source "Facebook / Instagram ads".
Google Ads → WhatsApp has no automatic id (a different pre-filled message per campaign is the tip).

**Facebook Messenger from ads** (`referral` on the first message) — same, when Meta sends it.

## How a source is named (shown in Hebrew and English)

| Seen                                                                                             | Source                        |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------- |
| `gclid`, or `utm_source=google` with `utm_medium` cpc/ppc/paid                                   | Google Ads                    |
| from google.\* without a click id                                                                | Google (search)               |
| `fbclid`/`utm_source` facebook·instagram with a paid medium, or a WhatsApp/Messenger ad referral | Facebook / Instagram ads      |
| from facebook.com / instagram.com otherwise                                                      | Facebook / Instagram          |
| `ttclid` or `utm_source=tiktok`                                                                  | TikTok                        |
| `msclkid` / bing                                                                                 | Microsoft Ads                 |
| any other `utm_source`                                                                           | that name (e.g. "newsletter") |
| another site                                                                                     | Other site (its host)         |
| nothing                                                                                          | Direct                        |

## Data

- `leads.attribution` (JSON, shared Zod schema): `{ source, medium?, campaign?, term?, content?,
clickIdType?, referrerHost?, landingPath?, adId?, adHeadline?, seenAt }` — never the click id's value
  beyond what's needed (kept for a later "report back to Google"), never personal data.
- `leads.attribution_source` (indexed) for filtering and reports.

## Screens

- **Lead page**: "Where it came from" in the details — source badge, campaign, keyword, landing page.
- **Leads list**: filter by source (and campaign when chosen).
- **Reports**: "Where your customers come from" — per source and per top campaigns: leads, became
  customers, deal value, close rate, for the chosen period.
- **Help**: "How to tag your ads" — turn on Google Ads auto-tagging; a UTM example for Facebook and
  other links; the WhatsApp tip.

## Not in this version

Reporting won deals back to Google Ads (offline conversions — needs Google's API approval), importing
ad spend (cost per lead/customer), call tracking numbers, sites that aren't built in LeadFlow.

## Acceptance criteria

1. A visit with `gclid`/UTMs, then the form on another page: the lead shows Google Ads and the campaign;
   a later ad click replaces an earlier one; a plain later visit doesn't.
2. Organic Google, another site, and direct visits are named correctly; no cookie value or click id is
   shown to other businesses or logged.
3. A WhatsApp lead from a Click-to-WhatsApp ad shows "Facebook / Instagram ads" and the ad headline.
4. Leads can be filtered by source; Reports show leads, customers, value and close rate per source and
   per campaign.
5. Old leads without attribution show "Unknown"; nothing breaks.
6. Hebrew and English; tenant isolation as everywhere.

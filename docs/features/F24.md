# F24 — Unified inbox (all channels, one screen)

Status: **Built** (first version, 2026-11-09). Decision: D-158. Builds on F12 (WhatsApp) and
F23 (Facebook Messenger); Instagram (later) joins the same inbox.

Every conversation — WhatsApp, Facebook Messenger, later Instagram — in one list, each opened and answered
on the same screen. The "שיחות" page becomes this inbox.

## Screen

- **Desktop, two panes.** Left: conversations, newest first — the person's name (or number), the channel's
  icon and colour, the last message's first line, its time, an **unread** dot. Filters at the top:
  **All · Unread · New** (senders not classified yet) and a channel filter (All · WhatsApp · Facebook).
  Right: the open conversation (the same bubbles as the lead page), a header with the name, the channel
  and **"Open lead ›"**, and the reply box with the 24-hour limit and the channel's "Send on …" button.
- **New senders** show **Business / Personal** in the conversation itself (the inbox cards go away).
  Replying to a new sender marks it Business (a lead is created), so a reply is never lost.
- **Phone (375 px):** the list; tapping opens the conversation full-screen with "‹ Back".
- **Tabs on the page:** Inbox (default) · Automatic reply · Personal numbers (as today).
- Live enough: the list and the open conversation refresh every 15 s (as now) and when the window gets
  focus. The sidebar's "Conversations" shows the unread count.
- Opening a conversation marks it read for the whole team (one shared state per conversation).

## Data & API

- A conversation is the existing sender record (`whatsapp_numbers`, channel-aware since F23). New columns:
  `last_message_at` (sorting, set on every message in or out) and `read_at` (unread = a customer message
  after it). Migration fills `last_message_at` from existing messages.
- `GET /businesses/:id/conversations?filter=all|unread|new&channel=…&cursor=…` — 30 per page.
- `GET /businesses/:id/conversations/:conversationId` — messages (oldest first, latest 200), channel,
  `replyUntil`, status, linked lead.
- `POST …/conversations/:conversationId/messages` — reply through the conversation's channel (24-hour
  rule; a new sender becomes Business first).
- `POST …/conversations/:conversationId/read` — marks it read.
- Classification reuses `POST …/whatsapp/numbers/:id/classify`. Personal numbers never appear.
- Permission: `whatsapp.inbox` (owners and managers), as the page today. Employees keep answering from
  the lead page of leads assigned to them.

## Not in this version

Instagram (separate feature, same inbox), assigning a conversation to a team member, saved replies,
attachments (still labels), search inside messages, typing/real-time push.

## Acceptance criteria

1. The inbox lists WhatsApp and Facebook conversations together, newest first, with channel, name, last
   message, time and unread; filters All / Unread / New and by channel work; personal numbers never show.
2. Opening a conversation shows its messages and marks it read for everyone; a new customer message
   makes it unread again and moves it to the top.
3. A reply goes out through the conversation's channel inside 24 hours, appears at once, and is refused
   after 24 hours with the channel's Hebrew explanation.
4. A new sender can be marked Business or Personal from the conversation; replying to a new sender marks
   it Business and creates the lead.
5. "Open lead" goes to the linked lead; the lead page's conversation tab still works.
6. Phone layout: list → conversation → back; keyboard: the list is navigable and the reply box reachable;
   loading, empty ("No conversations yet"), error and no-permission states; Hebrew and English.
7. Tenant isolation and permissions: outsiders 404 (route walker), employees no inbox (403), no message
   texts or phone numbers in logs.

## Effort

About 3–4 days: migration + API, the two-pane screen and phone layout, tests and e2e.
